Underlying boundary data derived from US Census Bureau TIGER/Line
shapefiles (public domain).

TopoJSON geometry, ids are 5-digit county FIPS codes matching this
project's own `fips` column (e.g. "01001"). File also bundles `states`
and `nation` objects in the same topology, useful for the state-level
drill-down view.
